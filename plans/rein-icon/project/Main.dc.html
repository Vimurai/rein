Three marks for Rein

Stroke-built, one colour plus one accent, drawn to read at 16 px. The ring is the bit — the one point where the reins meet the horse.

A — Taut
Two reins pulled in toward the bit ring. The whole product in one gesture. Recommended.
B — Loop R
A monogram: the R's leg is the rein, ending in the ring. Safest as an avatar; least story.
C — Bridle
The bit ring held from above by two reins. Symmetric, calm; can read as a pendant.
